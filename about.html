Our Story

Millionaires Choice Parfum is the creation of its Chairman and Founder, Mr George Jatania.

Mr Jatania has over 40 years of experience in the perfume and cosmetics industry. He founded Lornamead Ltd and, during his career, owned over 40 international brands, including the famous Yardley of London.

He created Millionaires Choice with French Master Perfumers. The collection is the result of years of passion and research, made to reflect a millionaire's lifestyle.
