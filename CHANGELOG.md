# Changelog

All notable changes to the MC Parfum website. Newest first.
Dates are 2026.

## [1.4.0] — 26 Nov 2026
### Changed
- Hero slider now moves **right to left** (next banner pushes in from the right). Back arrow and swipe go the other way. Speed 0.9 s, autoplay every 5 s.

## [1.3.0] — 26 Nov 2026
### Added
- Real policy pages from MC's own website: Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Shipping & Delivery, Return & Refund Policy, Cruelty-Free Promise (`policy.html`).
- Contact form now sends the message on WhatsApp.
- Google Map of the Dubai office (Capricorn Tower, Sheikh Zayed Road).
- Real founder story on the About page.
- Icon row with real facts: French Master Perfumers, Cruelty-Free, Free Travel Kit.
### Changed
- Footer rebuilt in Ajmal's layout (5 columns, white logo, social icons, email box, centered copyright).
- Winter banner is now the first hero slide.
- Hero slides change on their own (no pause on hover).
- Cart "Checkout" now sends the order on WhatsApp.
### Removed
- Extra category tab row on the collection page.
- Blogs section (MC has no blog posts).
- Placeholder reviews, account icon, brand-video and map placeholders.
- Every empty `#` link (31 internal links checked, all work).

## [1.2.0] — 26 Nov 2026
### Added
- 4 new hero banners made in Google Flow in Ajmal's style, with text built into the image: Winter, Our Signature, Trial Kit (AED 200), The Gift of Luxury.

- Collection page in Ajmal's layout (banner, filters, "Showing X out of Y", sort, 4-column grid).

- Header now scrolls away with the page (was sticky). Sits on top of the hero on the home page.
- Sizes matched to Ajmal: menu 14 px light with "|" dividers, icons 20 px, headings 24 px, cards on #fcf8ef.
